{# ---- Pipeline: how an event becomes intelligence ---- #}

How it works

  1. 01
    Collect

    Security events, desktop scanner findings, and manual analyses enter one queue.

  2. 02
    Detect

    Deterministic, explainable rules flag known patterns. A single suspicious string never proves exploitation.

  3. 03
    Reason

    CyberLLM (Qwen3-1.7B + v8 adapter) interprets ambiguity, explains evidence, and states uncertainty.

  4. 04
    Map & review

    Findings are validated against the local MITRE ATT&CK v20.0 dataset and queued for human review. Nothing is auto-approved, auto-blocked, or auto-remediated.
