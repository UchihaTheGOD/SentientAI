About Sentient

A community for people who write things down.

Sentient is a publishing community. People write essays, tutorials, notes, reviews and project write-ups here, and other people read them, respond to them and argue with them. That is the whole idea.

There is no algorithmic timeline deciding what deserves your attention. You follow the writers you want to hear from, browse the topics you care about, and the rest stays out of your way.

How it works

Everything on Sentient starts with someone writing a post.

  • Write — drafts are private until you publish them, and you can unpublish or archive anything you have second thoughts about.
  • Discover — posts are organised by topic and tag, with recent, popular and trending views so good writing does not sink.
  • Follow — following a writer puts their new posts in your feed. Nothing else does.
  • Discuss — comments and replies sit under each post. Likes are a signal, not a scoreboard.
  • Save — bookmark anything you want to come back to.

What we expect

The short version: write in good faith, and assume the same of everyone else.

  • Publish your own work, and credit the work of others.
  • Disagree with the argument, not the person making it.
  • No harassment, no spam, no impersonation.
  • Do not post other people's private information.

Posts and comments can be reported, and a reported item is reviewed by a human before anything happens to it. Moderation decisions are recorded.

Your account

An account needs a username, an email address and a password — that is all. Your email address is never shown on your profile or to other members. Your display name, bio, location and website are optional and are the only profile details anyone else can see.

You can change your password at any time from your account settings, and edit or clear any profile field from your profile.

Getting in touch

Questions, suggestions, or something broken? The contact form reaches us directly. If you are reporting a specific post or comment, use the report link on the item itself — it gives us the context we need to act.
